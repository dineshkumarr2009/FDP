What good looks like

    
The trainer's own box

    
This is the actual standing instruction behind the sessions you are learning from. Yours will look like this.

    

      
Dinesh Sir's custom instruction


Address me as Dinesh Sir.

I am an assistant professor of commerce in Bengaluru with 11
years of teaching experience. I teach digital marketing,
marketing management, company law, business law, creative
advertising, entrepreneurship and organisational behaviour.
Most of what I ask you to make is for B.Com and BBA students,
for whom English is a second language.

Keep responses short by default. If I need a long answer I
will ask for it in the prompt. When a topic has more than one
view, give me the neutral or balanced position as well as your
own, do not flatten it to one side. Use Indian examples,
brands and context wherever you can. Do not open with filler
like "great question", get to the point. If something is
unclear, make a sensible assumption and state it rather than
asking me a string of questions first.

Avoid heavy jargon without a plain-English gloss.

Last updated July 2026.

    

    
Notice what it does not say. It is not a wish list of twenty rules fighting each other. It is short, specific, and every line changes how the reply comes back. That is the whole craft.

  


  
  

    
Step two · paste it in

    
Where the box lives in each tool

    
Same paragraph, every tool. These menus move every few months, so follow the meaning, not just the exact words. If a label has changed, look for anything named personalization, custom instructions, or profile.


    

      
ChatGPTOpenAI

      

        
1Open Settings

        
2Go to Personalization

        
3Open Custom instructions and paste into the two boxes: who you are in the first, how you want replies in the second

      

      
Watch this. ChatGPT now also has a Personality dropdown and a Memory that learns on its own. Both can quietly override what you typed. If replies drift from your instruction, check those two are not fighting it.

    


    

      
      
ChatGPT · Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions

    


    

      
GeminiGoogle

      

        
1Open Settings, then Personal Intelligence

        
2Open Instructions for Gemini

        
3Paste your paragraph and save

      

      
The same screen has Memory, which learns from your past chats. Leave it on if you are comfortable, off if you would rather it did not. Your typed instruction works either way.

    


    

      
      
Gemini · Settings → Personal Intelligence → Instructions for Gemini

    


    

      
ClaudeAnthropic

      

        
1Open Settings, then Profile

        
2Find the personal preferences field, sometimes called custom instructions

        
3Paste your paragraph. It loads into every new chat automatically

      

      
Claude also has Styles, quick tone presets you switch per chat from the compose bar. Handy for flipping between formal and concise without editing your standing instruction.

    


    

      
Grok & DeepSeekthe newer ones

      

        
1Open Settings and look for Customize, Custom instructions or Personalization

        
2Paste the same paragraph if the box exists

      

      
These move fastest of all, and not every version has a standing-instruction box yet. If you cannot find one, no loss: just paste your paragraph at the top of an important chat instead.

    


    

      
NotebookLMthe honest exception

      

        
→There is no standing-instruction box, and that is the point. NotebookLM only ever answers from the sources you upload, so it does not need to be told who you are in general.

      

      
You steer it per notebook instead, with the customise line when you generate an audio, video or quiz. That is exactly why it cannot wander off topic the way a general chatbot can.
